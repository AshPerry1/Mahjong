What Is American Mahjong?

Unlike classic Chinese mahjong, American mahjong (often spelled mah jongg) uses a standardized annual card from the National Mah Jongg League (NMJL). Players match tiles in sets—pairs, pungs, kongs, and quintets—to complete a winning hand. It is strategic, social, and endlessly replayable—the perfect fun game for brunches, book clubs, sororities, and family gatherings.

Mahjong 101: Your First Game

In Mahjong 101, we cover everything a beginner needs:

  • Understanding mahjong tiles—bams, craks, dots, winds, dragons, and jokers
  • Reading the NMJL mahjong card and choosing a hand
  • Rules, Charleston, and table etiquette
  • Scoring basics and playing a complete game

We provide all tiles, tables, and materials—you just bring yourself and a friend group of 4–8 players.
