Mahjong 101

$125 per person

Master the fundamentals: tile recognition, game rules, scoring systems, and basic strategy. Complete 1-2 full games during your first session.

  • All materials provided
  • Hands-on gameplay experience
  • NMJL card included for the year
  • Post-session support

Mahjong 102

$115 per person

Advance your skills with advanced strategies, Siamese and Patio play techniques, and expert-level tactics to dominate the game.

  • 2-3 hour advanced session

  • Professional strategy guide
  • Ongoing support access

American Mahjong Lessons, Tiles & Events

From Lookout Mountain to The Greenbrier and beyond

Lookout Mountain Mahjong is your home for American mahjong—the beloved tile-and-card board game that brings friends, families, and clubs together. Certified TML Ambassadors Mahj Jen and Mahj Hen teach everything from first-time players to tournament-ready strategy, using beautiful pink, purple, and green mahjong tiles from The Mahjong Line.

Whether you are searching for mahjong lessons near me, planning a private party, or dreaming of playing at The Greenbrier Resort, we travel nationwide for corporate events, sorority gatherings, country clubs, and destination tournaments.
