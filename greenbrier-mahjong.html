American Mahjong at The Greenbrier Resort

The Greenbrier is synonymous with elegance, tradition, and gathering—and mahjong fits perfectly. Our team has hosted competitive tournaments and beginner-friendly sessions where guests learn the NMJL card, play with stunning pink, purple, and green mahjong tiles, and connect over one of the country’s favorite social board games.

Whether you are a lifelong player or picking up tiles for the first time, we design every Greenbrier experience around your group: structured instruction, mini tournaments, or a full weekend of competitive play.

What to Expect at a Greenbrier Mahjong Tournament

  • Professional instruction from certified TML Ambassadors
  • Premium mahjong tiles, tables, and NMJL cards provided
  • Formats for beginners through advanced tournament players
  • A social, fun atmosphere perfect for friends, clubs, and multi-generational groups

Why Lookout Mountain Mahjong?

We are not just instructors—we are community builders. From Lookout Mountain, Georgia to Martha’s Vineyard, Bristol, and The Greenbrier, Mahj Jen and Mahj Hen have taught thousands of players the art of American mahjong. Our Mahjong 101 and 102 lessons give beginners a clear path, while our tournament experience keeps seasoned players engaged.

Follow us on TikTok and Instagram for Greenbrier highlights, tile tips, and upcoming event announcements.
