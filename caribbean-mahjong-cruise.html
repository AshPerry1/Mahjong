Caribbean Mahjong Cruise

This Caribbean mahjong cruise is a six-night island itinerary with hosted American mahjong by Ann Henley Perry and Jen Kline. Sail from San Juan, snorkel St. John, explore Virgin Gorda’s Baths, stroll Grand Turk, and enjoy two sea days of play. Book this Caribbean mahjong trip through Martha King Travel only.

Caribbean mahjong cruise itinerary

  • San Juan, Puerto Rico — embark January 29, 2027
  • St. John, USVI — coral gardens of Virgin Islands National Park
  • Virgin Gorda, BVI — the famous Baths
  • Sailing day — spa, ocean views, and mahjong programming
  • Grand Turk, Turks and Caicos — historic streets and turquoise water
  • Sailing day — hosted play and strategy sessions
  • Miami, Florida — disembark

Sea days are built for mahjong: open play, friendly mini tournaments, and coaching with Lookout Mountain Mahjong. Full suite pricing and the $900 hosted experience are on the main 2027 mahjong cruise page. See also the 2027 mahjong cruise overview and Ilma yacht details.
