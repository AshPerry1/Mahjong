The Mahjong Instructors Going Viral from Lookout Mountain to The Greenbrier

Mountain Mahjong with Jen Kline (Mahj Jen) and Ann Henley Perry (Mahj Hen) — certified TML Ambassadors teaching American mahjong nationwide. Featured at The Greenbrier Resort, Martha’s Vineyard, and across Instagram at @lookoutmountainmahjong.

The Story in One Sentence

Two best friends turned certified mahjong ambassadors are making American mahjong the hottest social board game in the South—and taking their pink, purple, and green tiles from Lookout Mountain to luxury resorts like The Greenbrier.

Why It’s Trending

  • American mahjong is exploding among millennials, Gen X, and empty-nesters seeking screen-free social connection
  • Lookout Mountain Mahjong has built a fast-growing Instagram community at @lookoutmountainmahjong
  • They host tournaments at The Greenbrier Resort and travel nationwide for private lessons
  • Official ambassadors for The Mahjong Line (TML)—the premium tile brand behind the aesthetic mahjong movement
