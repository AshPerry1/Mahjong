Who Are Mahj Jen & Mahj Hen?

Best friends turned mahjong ambassadors, Jen Kline and Ann Henley Perry teach American mahjong from their home on Lookout Mountain, Georgia to luxury resorts like The Greenbrier, Martha’s Vineyard, and private events nationwide.

What They Teach

  • Mahjong 101 — beginner lessons from tiles to your first full game ($125/person)
  • Mahjong 102 — strategy, Siamese & Patio play ($115/person)
  • Corporate team building, sorority parties, country clubs
