What Is American Mahjong?

Mahjong (also spelled mah jongg) is a tile-and-card board game played with four people, a set of 152 tiles, and the annual NMJL card. Unlike Chinese mahjong, American mahjong uses jokers, the Charleston, and hundreds of winning hands that change every year—making it endlessly replayable and perfect for brunches, clubs, and luxury resorts.
